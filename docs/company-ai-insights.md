# Company-owner AI analytics

Owner-only companion to My team analytics. New route /company/ai-analytics. Existing team-lead APIs retain their original scope.

GET /api/v1/company-ai-insights/snapshot?days=7|30|90 returns whole-company operational metrics, equal previous-period comparison, all team and salesperson rows, daily lead/task trends, and current signed sale contracts. POST /api/v1/company-ai-insights accepts only {requestId,days,language}; GET on that path returns {run:null} or the latest private run for this owner/period/language. GET /:id returns its status/result. A company ID, team ID or employee filter cannot redefine server scope.

Access requires a company_owner in the company_admin pool with company scope, reports.read, leads.read, pipeline.read, tasks.read and contracts.read, a current CRM subscription, and no platform workspace inspection session. Tenant RLS applies to snapshots, jobs and all comparisons. Team leads and agents are denied even if handed a report UUID or reports.read permission.

Operational lead metrics exclude archived leads. All company owners, inactive/deleted users and unassigned records remain included in company totals. The salesperson comparison shows agent/team_lead roles and clearly marks inactive/deleted accounts. The no-team bucket includes records assigned to teamless users plus unassigned leads/tasks/contracts; unassigned work is separately counted. Current lead ownership, task assignees and contract owners determine current team attribution, not a historical roster. Won/lost figures are current lead stages changed during the period, not a cohort conversion history. Signed sales use currently signed sale contracts and signed dates, including contracts whose linked lead is archived; they are separate from lead opportunity values and cash collections.

Every team and salesperson is available in the comparison tables. Company AI uses all-company totals, up to ten teams (including the no-team bucket) and twenty salespeople selected by open workload; coverage is disclosed. The model receives anonymous team/member keys, statuses, roles, dates and metrics only—never names, email, phone, tenant/user/team IDs, lead wants, task text or notes. Thinking is enabled with a bounded two-minute request and low reasoning effort for this larger comparison. Evidence IDs and entity references are validated against the supplied snapshot. Narrative numeric characters are rejected; values are rendered from database evidence. Suggestions do not alter staff access, assignments or tasks.

Reports use durable private jobs, explicit generation, request-ID idempotency, ten-minute successful-result caching, one active request/user, ten paid attempts/hour/user and sixty/day/company. Job creation and completion are audited atomically; an audit failure prevents the paid call. No automatic paid retries. Interrupted requests expire after three minutes. Changed data/membership/roles/account status/period marks saved reports stale and hides their content. Regular metrics stay usable if AI is unavailable. API-process jobs do not resume automatically after restart.
