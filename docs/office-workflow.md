# Real estate office workflow

Enabled only for companies with `company_type=real_estate_office` and a current active subscription. Company owner/staff session responses expose `company.type`; platform inspection sessions expose `workspace.company.type`. Other company types retain their existing CRM and inventory workflow. Office clients and properties are company-private operational records, independent of the shared catalog and normal brokerage lead/pipeline tables. There were no active office companies or legacy office CRM records in the staging preflight; this release does not automatically copy or delete brokerage records.

## Access and behavior

- Existing Sales Agent, Team Lead and Company Owner roles are reused, including multi-role accounts. `leads.read/manage`, `units.read` and `reservations.read/manage` gate operations. Staff can add a property using their CRM write access even without the broader brokerage `units.manage` permission.
- All eligible CRM staff can see office property metadata, owner phones and property photos. New properties appear immediately. Only the responsible salesperson, company management or the current team lead of the responsible salesperson can edit them.
- Sales see only their own office clients, activities and deals, including search, recommendations, contract downloads and direct UUID access. Current team leads can inspect their current team's clients; company management sees all company clients. Team membership is rechecked from the database. A salesperson books only for their own client; management may book on their behalf. Closing/cancellation/renewal requires the deal owner or company management.
- A shared booked-property response includes the responsible salesperson and lease end, never an unauthorized client's name, ID or phone. Client reassignment moves linked deals to the new responsible salesperson atomically; audit/activity actors remain historical facts.
- Bookings have **no automatic expiry**. A company/property partial unique index permits one active booking/rental. Booking, cancellation, close and renewal use the company's CRM advisory lock, row versions, idempotency, audit and outbox in one transaction. Cancel requires a reason and restores availability. Sale closure sets the property sold; monthly rental closure sets it rented, with agreed monthly amount, calendar start/end and optional private PDF/HTTPS contract. No accounting, payment, payroll or commission record is created.

## API

Base `/api/v1/office`. Every write uses `{requestId: UUID, version?: integer, data: {...}}`. Existing records require their current version. Zod schemas in `office.schema.ts` validate nested payloads strictly, including real calendar dates, bounded Cairo local timestamps, positive listing prices and HTTPS URLs. Money is exact integer **EGP minor units**, never floating point. All leases are monthly, with a minimum calendar-month term from property settings; lease end is the date the property becomes available again.

- `GET options`: active CRM staff choices, current scope (`own/team/company`), `canManage`, `timeZone`.
- `GET clients|properties|deals`: server filters, total and pagination (24 default, 100 maximum). `GET clients/:id` includes the latest 100 private communication/viewing activities; `GET properties/:id` returns shared property metadata and permitted edit/booking flags.
- `POST clients`, `POST clients/:id`: name, normalized phone, optional email, source, stage, owner (within assignment scope), follow-up and requirements: sale/rent/both, preferred areas, type, furnishing, purchase/monthly rent budgets, minimum bedrooms/bathrooms, area range and move-in date. Office client phones are unique per company; duplicate errors disclose no owner/client record.
- `POST properties`, `POST properties/:id`: title, owner name/normalized phones, city/area/address, type, sale/rent/both, furnishing, rooms, bathrooms, area, floor, finishing, sale/monthly rent/security deposit, minimum lease months, available/last-verified dates, photos, description and negotiability. Availability edits are limited to available/withdrawn; booked/rented/sold states follow deals. Owner phone + address similarity produces a reviewable duplicate warning; an explicit reason allows a deliberate duplicate. Responsible salesperson is defaulted to the creator; reassignment is management-scoped.
- `POST clients/:id/activities`: call/note/viewing, private notes, optional outcome, property and Cairo-local scheduled/follow-up dates. A viewing requires a property and appointment; logging a call updates last-contact time.
- `POST bookings`: propertyId/propertyVersion, clientId, sale/rent purpose, optional agreement notes. Booked stock cannot be booked again. Availability and purpose are verified server-side.
- `POST deals/:id`: `action=cancel|close|renew`. Cancel requires reason; close requires positive agreed amount and rental start/end dates if applicable; renew extends the current rental end date and increments its lease revision. Optional uploaded/HTTPS contract and notes are supported. Old expiring notices are marked read on renewal.
- `GET clients/:id/recommendations?purpose=sale|rent`: deterministic office-stock matching with saved requirements and an exact `floor(budget*110/100)` maximum. Sale uses purchase budget/price; rent uses monthly budget/rent. Unknown prices, sold/withdrawn/booked properties and incompatible requirements are excluded. A rented property may be shown as available soon only if its lease ends by the requested move-in date; it cannot be booked before release. Hard filters apply before the 1,000-candidate work bound; returns up to 24 matches, price, above-budget amount/percentage and bilingual evidence. This built-in matcher works with AI disabled and makes no external provider request or charge. Existing Kimi/catalog matching remains unchanged for brokerage users.
- `GET filters?view=clients|properties`, `POST filters`: user-private saved filters. Lists filter on keyword, responsible staff, status, purpose, area, city, type, furnishing, price/budget range, bedrooms, bathrooms, area/floor ranges, source, finishing, creation dates, last-contact/verified dates, follow-up status, move-in/availability and leases ending within 15 days. Unknown filter keys and invalid ranges are rejected. UI groups extra filters and exposes active filters/reset/sorting.
- `POST media` multipart: requestId, kind=photo|contract, file, clientId for contracts. Photos: PNG/JPEG/WebP; contract: PDF, up to 5 MB per file; magic bytes verified, 100 MB company media cap. Files are immutable PostgreSQL bytea records in the existing persistent database. `GET media/:id` rechecks company/client scope, uses private/no-store and nosniff; PDF downloads are attachments. Property photo lists are capped at 20. Original local filenames are not retained; unused uploaded files count against the cap.

## Lease automation

`runOfficeSweep` runs every minute in the existing automation worker, independently of the Finance module flag. Active subscribed office companies are enumerated through a restricted platform view. Cairo calendar dates determine eligibility. Within 15 days of lease end, all current active sales/team leads/company managers receive an in-app notification containing property code and end date, without client details. On the lease-end date the transaction marks the lease ended, makes the property available and notifies the office again. Bookings never participate in expiry.

Notifications are deduplicated by company/deal/lease revision/kind; renewals schedule a fresh revision. Batch selection excludes deliveries already recorded so later batches cannot starve. Failed company transactions roll back availability, notification ledger and audit together and are retried; other companies continue. Office failures do not stop existing Finance automation. Offline/failed worker processing catches up on its next successful sweep; no browser needs to remain open. No outgoing email/SMS is sent by this workflow.

## Dashboard and validation

Office menu: overview, clients, office properties, bookings and deals, plus the existing catalog and applicable HR/administration tools. No Finance or Commissions step appears. Client creation is available to office sales; other company sales retain the prior hidden New lead control. Property and contract links are tenant-scoped; global search returns permitted office records.

## Office cities and owner phone

Cities belong to each office company, not the platform catalog. Only its authenticated Company Owner can open `/company/office/cities` and use `/api/v1/office/cities` to create, rename, soft-delete and restore its cities. Sales and team leads receive their company's choices through office options only. Platform and inspection sessions cannot manage them. The old platform city administration is retired.

New offices start with no cities. Migration072 copies only cities referenced by each company's existing properties, preserving IDs and names, then retires writes to the old global directory (legacy read grants remain for deployment compatibility; no new endpoint uses them). Company RLS, composite city/property foreign keys and company-scoped names prevent cross-office reads and references. Writes use actor-bound idempotency, versions and transactional tenant auditing. Deleted cities cannot be chosen for new properties; existing properties may retain them and their historical name snapshots.

Property forms submit a city ID resolved by the server within the company. Filters accept the stable ID. Owner phones accept exactly one Egyptian number (`+20` followed by9–10 national digits, without a leading zero). The form uses a fixed +20 prefix and a single telephone input. Historical phone arrays are not truncated by migrations.

Office Team invitations and explicit role changes accept only Sales Agent and Team Lead. An existing Employee role remains internal when editing sales roles; other company types retain full role selection. Existing accounts are not bulk modified.

## Tasks, calendar and contacts

Real estate offices retain the shared Tasks, Calendar and Contacts screens at `/company/tasks`, `/company/calendar` and `/company/contacts`. Contacts keep their existing company isolation and personal ownership. The office Contacts route is no longer redirected to Clients.

In office workspaces the existing task/event `leadId` field selects an office client; it is persisted in a separate `office_client_id` column with a composite company foreign key, never inserted into the brokerage lead foreign key. Responses retain the compatible `lead` shape with bilingual name and `kind: office_client`. The UI opens `/company/office/clients?clientId=...`. Brokerage workspaces retain their original lead links. Generic unlinked work is available in either workspace, while linked work is scoped to the active workspace family.

Sales see their assigned tasks and owned events and link only their accessible clients/contacts. Current team leads see their active team's work and may assign tasks/events to current team members. Linked client work assigned to another user must match that client's responsible salesperson. Every read/write rechecks current team membership and versions within the transaction. When a client/contact moves outside a viewer's scope, task/event responses hide its live identity/link, including search and picker results. Calendar contact choices respect existing personal contact ownership. Existing task status, event status, idempotency, audit and subscription rules remain in effect.
