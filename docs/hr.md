# HR foundation

Company-private employee records support all staff, including employees without sales roles. The owner and authorized HR staff open `/company/hr/employees`, `/departments`, `/documents`. Existing Team screens continue to manage application accounts and sales teams. Saving a current employee without a linked login requires an email and atomically creates an Employee-only login with an emailed activation link. Linking an existing same-company account preserves its roles and password. HR job titles do not assign CRM roles. Existing linked logins cannot be replaced or detached through an HR edit.

## Access and storage

All `/api/v1/hr` endpoints require a current company subscription and either the company-wide company_owner principal with workspace.view, or a company_user with an assigned HR Officer/HR Manager role and hr.read (hr.manage for writes). Platform inspection sessions are rejected. Salespeople, team leads, Employee and Payroll Officer cannot access HR unless also assigned an authorized HR role. HR operations explicitly use company scope; combining HR with sales never broadens the user’s personal CRM scope. Every reference has a composite company foreign key, every lookup contains company_id, employee, department and document tables force tenant RLS. No public file URLs exist. Downloads require the same owner authorization, are served as attachments with no-store and write a download audit event. HR files are separate from CRM documents and inaccessible through CRM document endpoints.

Files: non-empty PDF/PNG/JPEG/UTF-8 text up to 5 MiB, content/extension inspected by the existing file validator. Bytes are stored privately in PostgreSQL. CRM plus HR documents (including archived files) count toward the existing company storage limit. Uploads serialize on the same company lock as CRM document uploads. Archive/restore is recoverable and does not release storage. Existing backups must include the database. No automatic document email, public sharing or virus-scanning claim is made.

## Contract

GET `/overview`: employee/active/withoutCrm/department/document-expiry counts. GET `/options`: departments, first 1000 employee and CRM-user reference choices. Use directory search/pagination for records beyond the chooser cap.

GET `/employees`, `/departments`, `/documents`: `{items,total,page,limit}`. Strict query: q (up to160 chars), page, limit (1–100; default25), status, departmentId, employeeId, archived=false|true|all. Status/department filters apply to employees; employee/archive filters apply to documents. Department and employee-name searches supported.

POST `/employees[/:id]`: requestId UUID, employeeCode (optional legacy field; generated by the server on create, immutable on update), name, email (required for current employees without a linked login), phone, jobTitle, departmentId, managerId, userId, workLocation, employmentType (full_time/part_time/contract/intern), status (active/on_leave/offboarded), joiningDate, endDate, notes. Null clears optional references/dates/contact email; a linked userId is preserved if omitted; explicit detachment or replacement is rejected. Full replacement semantics: omitted optional fields reset to defaults. Updates require current version. Manager cycles and offboarded manager assignments are rejected. Dates must be real calendar dates; end cannot precede joining. No hard deletion; offboarded records remain searchable. New employees default to Active; no onboarding checklist is created.

POST `/departments[/:id]`: requestId, name, description, archived, version on update. Active names unique case-insensitively. Move current employees before archiving. Offboarded historical employees may retain an archived department.

POST `/documents`: multipart file, requestId, employeeId, title, kind=contract|identity|certificate|other, optional expiresDate. GET `/documents/:id/download`. POST `/documents/:id/archive`: requestId, version, archived boolean. Lists return metadata, never bytes. Expired/expiringSoon flags use Africa/Cairo calendar date and a30-day window.

Writes use requestId-based idempotency and current version for edits. Reusing a request ID with another actor/body is409. Company-level transactional serialization prevents duplicates/races. Audit/outbox records contain action and resource ID only; employee notes, document bytes and personal fields are excluded. Failed audit rolls back the entire write.

## Verification

`npm test` includes real HTTP requests against the Nest app and isolated PostgreSQL-compatible database with all migrations/RLS: cross-company references, permissions/inspection denial, Employee-only account creation, idempotency, version conflicts, manager cycles, dates, private downloads, archive/restore, file validation and audit rollback. Frontend tests cover actual data rendering, save payloads, failure recovery, retired onboarding navigation and multipart uploads. No fake employees are automatically seeded into existing companies.

Employee onboarding was retired in migration053. Historical checklists remain stored but runtime access is revoked, new staff default to active, and the employee directory no longer creates or displays checklist tasks. Existing company logins receive Employee as an additional role without changing their primary role, scope, status or sales team. Platform administrator accounts are excluded. Migration054 repairs missing login-backed staff profiles. New company staff accounts create a linked HR profile in the same invitation transaction. Existing same-company profiles are retained; exactly one matching email is linked without replacing employment details. Ambiguous email matches remain for explicit HR review. Company owners remain separate unless HR explicitly creates their staff record.

Login-backed profiles have a stable generated employee code, the existing account name/email, and default Active employment status. Department, manager, job title and employment dates remain unset until HR enters them. Disabling a login does not automatically offboard staff. No welcome emails or password changes are triggered by the repair. Creation/linking writes a transactional system audit/outbox event; failed auditing rolls back the account invitation.

Employee codes are assigned by a database trigger from a company-isolated transactional counter: EMP-0001, EMP-0002 and onward without truncation past9999. The same allocator covers HR-created and login-backed records. Legacy identifiers remain unchanged; existing numeric EMP codes seed the counter and collisions are skipped. Client-supplied codes on create are ignored for compatibility. Updates may omit the code or send its unchanged value; renaming a code is rejected. Request replay reuses the saved response, and failed creation rolls back its allocation. The form shows a read-only placeholder until save.

## Optional company module

Platform company create/update/draft inputs accept boolean `hrEnabled`; company responses and owner/staff session `company` include it. New companies default to false. Migration057 preserves enabled HR for all existing companies. Admins can change the setting later using the company editor and current version; changes are audited. Older update clients omitting the field preserve its value.

When disabled, all HR and leave APIs (including private file downloads, My leave, manager approvals, policies and balances) return403 `hr_module_disabled`. Services check persisted configuration on every operation. Navigation and direct routes hide/block these pages. CRM roles, invitations, login access and automatic linked employee profiles keep working. Disabling retains HR records and leave history; re-enabling restores access under the normal role permissions.

## Employee access and activation

Migration058 adds an encrypted activation-email queue and a narrow tenant provisioning bridge. Current employees created/saved without a login get the Employee role only, an invitation valid for24 hours, and a queued email from info@mujez.net. The existing activation page lets them set their own password. Accounts remain invited until activation. Offboarded records do not get new logins. Existing records without an email must be completed before login can be created; identities are never invented. Existing logins are never reset by saving HR details. No mass emails are sent during migration.

POST `/api/v1/hr/employees/:id/login-invitation` accepts requestId and version. Authorized HR staff/owner can requeue a pending invitation or provision an eligible unlinked record. Renewing revokes unused old links; active/disabled logins are not reset. Queue payloads encrypt the token with account-specific generation binding to the employee. Sending uses the configured SMTP worker with leases, bounded retries and token removal on acceptance, terminal failure or cancellation. Responses, logs and idempotency results contain no activation token. Queue status appears as loginDelivery; loginEmail is the actual login identifier, separate from HR contact email.

GET `/api/v1/employee/me` permits company_user identities with Employee and leave.self, returning only their own employee code, name, job title, department, manager, joining date and status. HR notes and private files are excluded. With HR disabled it returns `{hrEnabled:false,employee:null}` while login/account services remain available. Employee-only accounts land on `/company/employee`; sales and HR administration require additional roles. My leave remains subject to the HR module and existing personal authorization rules.
